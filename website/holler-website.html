What runs locally

Built around service, not connectivity.

How Holler works

One outlet. Two layers. No drama.

The outlet runs locally. The cloud gives owners visibility and backup. The two reconnect automatically after an outage.

STEP 01

Install on Windows

Holler runs as a native application on the outlet's own machine. No browser tab is required for the counter.

STEP 02

Run locally all day

Billing, KOTs, staff access and kitchen screens continue inside your restaurant even during an ISP outage.

STEP 03

Reconnect and sync

When internet access returns, queued records move to the cloud automatically and owner dashboards catch up.

Local-first by design

The difference shows up when something fails.

This is not an argument against cloud software. Holler simply keeps the critical restaurant path independent of internet availability.
