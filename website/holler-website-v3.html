Why local-first

The cloud should help your restaurant. It should not hold it hostage.

Holler separates the work that must happen immediately inside the outlet from the work that benefits from the cloud. Billing and kitchen operations stay local. Central visibility and consolidation use the cloud when connectivity is available.
